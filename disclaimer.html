Taxation statutes in India, particularly the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act), Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (IGST Act), and Income Tax Act, 1961, are subject to frequent legislative amendments, Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) circulars, and GST Council notifications.
