Professional Focus

Areas of Practice

The following reflects the principal areas of professional practice. Each area is handled within the advocate's actual professional competence and enrolment.

GST Registration & Return Filing

Assistance with GST registration, monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings, annual GSTR-9 returns, and input tax credit (ITC) reconciliation.

Income Tax & ITR Filing

Income tax return preparation and e-filing for individuals, proprietorships, firms, and companies, along with Form 26AS/AIS/TIS verification.

Taxation Dispute Cases & Notice Handling

Representation and reply drafting for tax notices including Section 143(1) intimations, Section 143(2) scrutiny, Section 148 reassessment, and GST ASMT-10 / DRC-01 notices.

Tax Assessments, Appeals & Compliance

Assistance with rectification under Section 154, revised returns under Section 139(5), statutory records maintenance, and appeal preparation before tax authorities.

The above is a general description of practice areas. Specific availability of assistance in any matter should be confirmed directly with the advocate's office. Nothing on this website constitutes legal advice.

Professional Background

About the Advocate

Tasleem Arif has been practising as an advocate in Delhi for over a decade, focusing his legal work on taxation matters including Income Tax Returns (ITR), Goods and Services Tax (GST), tax compliance, and representation in tax dispute proceedings.

He holds academic degrees in B.Sc, B.Ed, and LLB, and is enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi. His court chamber is situated at Seat No. 10, near Gol Canteen, Tis Hazari Court, and his actual office is located at Gali No. 10, Sangam Vihar, Wazirabad, Delhi.

His professional assistance encompasses ITR e-filing across categories, GST return filings and reconciliations, responding to tax scrutiny and demand notices, and advising on ongoing statutory compliance.

He also operates from his office in Sangam Vihar, Wazirabad, Delhi — details of which are available on the Contact & Office page.

Professional Conduct Tasleem Arif practises in accordance with the Bar Council of India Rules and the applicable rules of professional conduct governing advocates in India.
