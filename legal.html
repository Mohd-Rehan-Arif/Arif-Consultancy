Taxation Disputes & Legal Proceedings

Professional representation and legal notice handling for Income Tax and GST dispute cases before statutory authorities in Delhi.

Notice of Practice Scope: Advocate Tasleem Arif's practice is focused specifically on taxation law (Income Tax & GST), return filing, tax dispute cases, and compliance. The contents below provide factual descriptions of taxation legal procedures, without any claim of specialist exclusivity or outcome guarantees.

Legal Tax Proceedings

Tax Dispute Representation

Tax disputes arise when there is an alleged discrepancy between filed returns, auto-populated data, and departmental assessments. Advocate Tasleem Arif represents taxpayers before tax authorities and appellate forums in Delhi.

Direct Tax

Income Tax Notice & Dispute Cases

Legal examination, document verification, and response drafting for Income Tax statutory proceedings:

  • Defective return notices under Section 139(9) of the Income Tax Act
  • Scrutiny assessment notices under Section 143(2) and faceless questionnaire replies
  • Reassessment notices and show-cause proceedings under Section 148 and Section 148A
  • Demand notices under Section 156 and penalty notices under Section 270A / 271
  • Rectification applications under Section 154 for apparent mistakes of law or fact
Note: Every income tax proceeding requires careful examination of Form 26AS, AIS, TIS, bank statements, and filed ITR acknowledgments.
Indirect Tax

GST Notice & Audit Disputes

Legal representation and factual reconciliation for Goods and Services Tax compliance disputes:

  • Scrutiny of return discrepancies under Form GST ASMT-10
  • Summary of demand and pre-notice intimations under DRC-01 / DRC-01A
  • Show Cause Notices (SCN) for input tax credit (ITC) mismatches between GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B
  • GST registration cancellation proceedings and revocation applications
  • Handling of audit queries and departmental inspections under the CGST Act
Note: GST disputes require exact reconciliation between purchase registers, e-way bills, tax invoices, and supplier filings.
